Welcome To Car Rental Portal

This Car Rental Portal Management System is designed for a web services provider. Users can visit the platform to book their desired car online, selecting the car, duration, and other options. The system includes a comprehensive car rental portal management system, allowing the admin to easily add, update, or delete any car and check user details. The primary purpose of this project is to increase client accessibility, as customers living far from showrooms can also book online and have the car delivered to their doorstep. The technologies used in this project include HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap for the frontend, and Python Django with SQLite for the backend.
